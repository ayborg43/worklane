# Odoo Features

Odoo is a suite of integrated business apps sharing one database/ORM, so data flows between them (e.g., a sales order can drive inventory, accounting, and manufacturing automatically).

## Sales & CRM
- CRM (leads, pipelines, opportunities)
- Sales (quotations, sales orders, subscriptions)
- Point of Sale (POS, retail/restaurant checkout, offline-capable)
- Rental (rental contracts and scheduling)

## Website & eCommerce
- Website builder (drag-and-drop)
- eCommerce (online store, cart, checkout)
- Blog, Forum, eLearning
- Live Chat
- Email Marketing / SMS Marketing / Social Marketing / Marketing Automation
- Events (registration, ticketing)

## Finance
- Accounting/Invoicing (multi-currency, tax handling, bank sync/reconciliation)
- Expenses
- Spreadsheet/Documents (with Sign for e-signatures)

## Supply Chain
- Inventory (warehouses, multi-step routes, barcode support)
- Purchase (RFQs, vendor management)
- Manufacturing (MRP: bills of materials, work orders, PLM)
- Maintenance
- Quality

## Human Resources
- Employees (HR records)
- Recruitment
- Time Off, Attendances, Appraisals
- Payroll (region-dependent)
- Referrals, Fleet

## Productivity/Services
- Project (task/project management, Gantt)
- Timesheets
- Field Service
- Helpdesk
- Knowledge (internal wiki)
- Approvals
- Discuss (internal chat/messaging)

## Platform/Technical Features (cuts across all apps)
- Studio: no-code customization of views, fields, workflows
- Custom module development (Python + XML views + OWL/JS frontend)
- Multi-company, multi-currency, multi-language
- Built-in workflow/automation rules, activities, and scheduled actions
- Reporting/dashboards per app plus a generic pivot/graph view builder
- REST/XML-RPC/JSON-RPC APIs for external integration
- Odoo.sh / on-premise / Odoo Online deployment options
- Community (free, open-source) vs Enterprise (paid, adds Studio, advanced accounting/HR features, support)
